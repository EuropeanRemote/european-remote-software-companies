# Contributing

Thank you for helping keep this list accurate. The table in README.md is generated from the data behind [europeanremote.com](https://europeanremote.com), so an accepted change appears both on the site and here.

## What we list

- Software companies that hire people in Europe for remote work, fully remote or hybrid.
- We prefer product companies, which build their own software, over agencies and outsourcing firms.
- We don't list gambling, cryptocurrency or adult content companies.
- The company needs a public website and a careers page or job board.

We check every company once a month. Companies that shut down, were absorbed into another company, or no longer run their own website are removed.

## Add a company

Choose whichever way suits you:

- Open an issue with the [company form](https://github.com/EuropeanRemote/european-remote-software-companies/issues/new?template=company.yml).
- Open a pull request that adds one row to the table in README.md, in alphabetical order. Leave "Hiring now" empty, because we fill it in during the monthly check. Put the details below in the pull request description.
- Use the form at [europeanremote.com/submit](https://europeanremote.com/submit), or write to hello@europeanremote.com.

Please include:

- Company name and website
- Careers page or job board
- Headquarters country, or "No office" for a fully distributed company
- Work mode: fully remote or hybrid
- Countries the company hires from
- Tech stack: back-end, front-end, databases and cloud
- One or two plain sentences about what the company makes
- A public link for each claim, for example the careers page that describes the remote policy

## Correct or remove an entry

Open an issue or a pull request with a link that shows the change, such as a new careers page or an acquisition announcement. Company staff can also ask us by email to update or remove a listing.

## Review

We review every change by hand in our spare time, so it can take a few weeks. An issue or pull request with sources is the quickest to review. We keep descriptions short and factual, so we may shorten the text you send.
